Citation Density & Rank Drift

Comparing Availability (how often a rank appears in SERP) vs. Selection (how often Gemini cites it).

What is Rank Drift?
If Gemini picked links randomly, the "Cited %" would match the "SERP %" (approx 3.3% for each rank). A positive Drift (e.g., +40%) means Gemini is "over-indexing" on that rank, showing a strong positional preference.
